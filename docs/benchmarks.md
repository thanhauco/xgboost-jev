# Benchmark results

Generated by `python -m xgboost_jev.bench` on 2026-10-19 (Python 3.13.16, xgboost 3.4.1, x86_64, single-threaded XGBoost).

> **Read this first.** All data is synthetic (`mock_data.py`) and Jev is the offline `MockJevClient` lexicon stand-in with a simulated 25 ms round trip. The numbers show how the pipelines behave relative to each other; they are not a measurement of the real Jev service or of production traffic. Re-run against a real Jev endpoint and your own labelled data before drawing conclusions.

## 3. Advanced fraud stack

`generate_advanced_stream`, 500 users over 60 days. Models train at **day 35** using only the labels *known by then* and are evaluated on days 35-60. The money-mule ring starts on day 38, after training, to test drift. Recall is measured at a fixed **1.5% alert budget** (review capacity). Tables show **mean ± standard deviation over 3 generator seeds** (21, 22, 23); per-scenario columns are means. Seed 21 (first-seed detail below): 74,027 transactions, 27,803 account events, 762 fraud; the cut sees only 46 of 385 pre-cut frauds labelled; 31,125 evaluation transactions, 377 fraud (account_takeover 11, app_scam 12, ato_warmup 13, card_testing 21, descriptor_spoof 5, impossible_travel 4, low_and_slow 170, mule_ring 141).
